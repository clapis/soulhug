Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a workshop?

Simply click on the workshop listed in our agenda. You’ll be redirected to the event page on Hipsy, where you can read the full description and book your spot directly.

What should I bring with me?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move and relax in. Bring a water bottle, a journal and pen, and anything that helps you feel cozy. If you’d like, you can also bring some favourite art materials, but everything essential will be available to share.

Can I offer a workshop as a gift? Do you offer private workshops or collaborations?

Yes - we love creating special experiences :-) Whether you would like to offer a workshop as a gift, organise a private session for a small group, or collaborate for a company or retreat event, we’re happy to discuss the possibilities. Just reach out via our contact form or via email hello@soulhug.nl.

I’m shy or not very expressive - will I still feel comfortable?

These sessions are designed as safe, gentle spaces where everyone comes as they are. You can decide how comfortable you feel with that type of environment, but just know that there is no pressure to perform or share anything - just an invitation to explore at your own pace.

Are your workshops suitable if I’m going through a difficult emotional period?

Our workshops can offer a soothing space to reconnect with yourself and express emotions creatively. However, they are not a substitute for therapy. If you’re currently navigating deep trauma, PTSD, or acute emotional distress, we recommend working with a qualified therapist alongside.

What makes this approach different from traditional yoga or art therapy?

Our sessions combine mindful movement and creative exploration in a unique way. Rather than focusing on technique or analysis, the aim is to explore how your body, breath, and creativity naturally connect - helping you feel more present, grounded, and alive.
