Your first visit

Do I need art experience?

Not at all. Bring your curiosity, and I'll guide you along the way. You don't need to know how to draw or paint, and there's nothing to get right. If you join a yoga and art workshop, the movement is gentle and beginner-friendly too.

Can I come on my own?

Of course. You're very welcome just as you are, whether you come with a friend or take this moment for you. If joining on your own feels a little daunting, that's understandable. There's no pressure to perform or share anything; you can settle in and explore at your own pace.

How do I book?

Find an upcoming session that speaks to you, then follow its link to read the details and book your place. For weekly yoga, book directly with the studio. If you're unsure which session to choose, send me a little note. I'd be happy to help.

What if I need to cancel?

Plans change, and I understand. For most workshops, you can cancel up to 14 days before the session for a full refund. Special events may have different conditions, so please check the details when booking. You'll find everything in the full cancellation terms.
