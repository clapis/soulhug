The Soul Hug Ateliers

Creative exploration

Creativity can be a gentle way to get to know yourself. With a theme to guide you, explore drawing, collage, writing, and other materials, letting colour, shape, and imagery give expression to thoughts and feelings that can be hard to put into words.

There is time to pause, look at what you've made, and notice what it brings up for you. In a supportive, non-judgmental space, follow your curiosity and discover the meaning or insights that emerge, without needing to explain or analyse everything.

No art skills needed. The value is in the exploring, not in making a perfect piece.

Yoga & art

Movement meets making

Bring together the stillness of yoga and the freedom of creative expression. Gentle movement and attention to your breath invite you to slow down, feel grounded, and listen to your body, making room to explore through art.

Guided by the session's theme, let the sensations, emotions, or images you notice find expression through colour, shape, and materials. With space to create and reflect, explore how movement and making can support each other, rather than focusing on a perfect pose or a finished artwork.

No yoga or art experience needed. Move, create, and reflect at your own pace, with no pressure to share.

A few practical things

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable clothes. Bring water, a journal, and a pen. Essential art materials are available to share; check your event description for any specific requests.

Do I have to share my work or feelings?

No. You can choose what you share and take part at your own pace. There is no pressure to explain your artwork or perform for the group.

Are these sessions therapy?

No. These are creative wellbeing workshops, not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're experiencing trauma or acute emotional distress, seek support from a qualified therapist. You can email me to discuss whether a session is a good fit.

Can I arrange a private workshop or a gift?

Get in touch about a gift, a private group, or a collaboration. We'll discuss the possibilities together: hello@soulhug.nl.
