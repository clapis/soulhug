    1. Introduction

    Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website, contact us, or book an activity or workshop. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Dutch privacy legislation.

    2. What Personal Data We Collect

    We may collect the following personal data:

    • Contact details: name, email address, phone number (if provided)

    • Booking details: workshop/activity booked, payment information

    • Communication: information you provide via the contact form or email

    • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and general website usage (via analytics tools, if used)

    3. How We Use Your Data

    We use your personal data for the following purposes:

    • To process and confirm bookings

    • To communicate with you about workshops, activities, or inquiries

    • To send relevant updates or newsletters (only with your consent)

    • To comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax administration)

    • To improve our website and services

    4. Legal Basis for Processing

    We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds under the GDPR:

    • Contractual necessity: to provide the services you booked

    • Legal obligation: to comply with tax and accounting requirements

    • Legitimate interest: to improve services and communication

    • Consent: for sending newsletters or marketing communications

    5. Data Retention

    • Booking and payment data: kept for 7 years (tax law requirement)

    • Newsletter subscription data: kept until you unsubscribe

    We do not sell or trade your personal data. Data may be shared only with:

    • Service providers (e.g., booking or payment systems, email services) who process data on our behalf under GDPR-compliant agreements

    • Authorities if legally required

    Our website may use cookies or analytics tools to improve user experience. You will be informed and asked for consent where required.

    You have the right to:

    • Access your data

    • Correct your data

    • Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”)

    • Restrict or object to processing

    • Withdraw consent at any time

    • Data portability (receive a copy of your data in a portable format)

    To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@soulhug.nl. We will respond within 30 days.

    9. Data Security

    We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

    10. Contact and Complaints

    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at hello@soulhug.nl.
