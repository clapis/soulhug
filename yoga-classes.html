Give Yourself a Moment to Slow Down

Nurture your body and mind in a loving and welcoming space. Join our weekly yoga classes for every body. A space to ease off, meet yourself with gentleness, listen inward, and move with presence.

Whether you seek to improve flexibility, reduce stress, or deepen your practice, our sessions are designed to meet your needs in a supportive environment.

Your facilitator

Valérie Rossi

Discovering yoga as a teenager, I rekindled my practice in 2018 when I longed for more softness, slowness and authenticity.

Whether in karate, dance or yoga, movement has always been a coming home, detaching from conditioned thoughts and tapping into inner truth - feeling the presence of the soul in the body and embodying a more assured and joyful version of myself.

Keen to share my passion with others, I trained at the renowned Arhanta Yoga Ashram in 2022 and earned a 200-hour Hatha Yoga Teacher certification. My teaching style is focused, enthusiastic, and kind-hearted, infused with invitations to slow down and pay attention to bodily sensations, as well as gentle encouragements to challenge yourself in a mindful way. You will often hear me emphasise the importance of finding steadiness and comfort in each yoga pose (body, mind and breath).

My aim is to assist others on their own journey of self-discovery and health, promoting a slower pace and the cultivation of awareness. Read more about our mission.
